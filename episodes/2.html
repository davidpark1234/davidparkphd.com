Is AI Killing the PhD?

About this episode

Recorded November 7, 2026.

This year, nearly three in ten science and engineering PhD dissertations at American universities contain writing that an AI produced. Back in 2023, it was fewer than two in a hundred. So is AI killing the PhD?

In this episode I look at the new NBER study behind that number; at the Google AI system whose top idea matched, in about two days, the answer an Imperial College lab spent about ten years on; and at a test where today's best AI models outscore PhD experts. Then I look at why universities are actually closing PhD seats this year, and I tell you which part of the PhD I think AI is killing, and which part is worth more now.

Related episode, the money side of AI: Is the AI Bubble Popping? youtu.be

Here's my question for you. Say a student lets AI write most of the dissertation, but the discovery in it is theirs and they ran every experiment. Did they earn the PhD? Yes or no, and give me your best reason in the comments.
