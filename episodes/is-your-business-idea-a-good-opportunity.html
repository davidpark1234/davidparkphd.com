Episode 1

Is Your Business Idea a Good Opportunity?

Most people think the hard part of starting a business is coming up with the idea. It's almost exactly backwards.

Coming to YouTube soon

About this episode

Through Maya, who wants to cook dinners for families with a new baby, this episode shows a different question to ask about any idea. What would have to be true for it to work? Then, for each of those claims, how good does it look, and how do you know?

It ends with MERIT, five plain questions you can use on any business, and a short exercise. You don't need any other episode to use them.

The five MERIT questions

For each one, write down what would have to be true, how good it looks, and how you know.

  1. M
    MarketDo enough people want it, badly enough?
  2. E
    EdgeWhy would they pick yours over what they do now?
  3. R
    ReturnsWill it make enough money to be worth it?
  4. I
    ImplementationCan you build it and deliver it?
  5. T
    TeamAre you the right people to do it?
