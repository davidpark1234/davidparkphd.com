Why a Good Idea Isn't Enough

A good idea and a good opportunity are two different things. Here's how to tell them apart, before you've bet too much on it.

Coming to YouTube soon

About this episode

In 2020, Quibi launched with $1.75 billion and leaders from the top of Hollywood and Silicon Valley, and about six months later it announced it was shutting down. Before the money went in, nobody had answered the one question it all rested on.

This episode asks what would have to be true for an idea to work, and how sure you really are of each part. It runs Quibi through five questions, MERIT, then does the same for Maya, who wants to cook dinners for families with a new baby, and ends with a short exercise for your own idea.

The five MERIT questions

For each one, write down what would have to be true, how good it looks, and how you know.

  1. M
    MarketDo enough people want it, badly enough?
  2. E
    EdgeWhy would they pick yours over what they do now?
  3. R
    ReturnsWill it make enough money to be worth it?
  4. I
    ImplementationCan you build it and deliver it?
  5. T
    TeamAre you the right people to do it?
