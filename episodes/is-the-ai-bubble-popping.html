Is the AI Bubble Popping?

About this episode

Recorded November 5, 2026.

Last Thursday, Alphabet, Google's parent company, lost nearly $500 billion in a single day, its worst day since it went public in 2004. A week later, on November 5, AI stocks fell together and the Nasdaq slid into a correction. So is the AI bubble popping?

In this episode I measure the sell-off against the line one big study uses for a crash, look at what Big Tech is spending on AI and what companies say they're getting back, compare it with the dot-com crash, and hold chip stocks up to the warning signs that came before past crashes. Then I tell you what I think it means for your retirement account and your career.

So what do you think? Is the AI bubble popping, or is this just a bad few weeks? Leave me your thoughts in the comments.
