Should We Shut Down Business Schools?

About this episode

Should we shut down business schools? Christopher Marquis, a professor who has taught at Harvard Business School, Cornell and Cambridge, argued in The New York Times that we should. I teach at a business school myself, so I've got skin in this game.

In this episode I lay out his case: students who arrive wanting to bring clean energy to market and leave chasing consulting and finance jobs, Harvard cases that praised Enron, and a study that found pay fell at companies that switched to a CEO with a business degree. Then I give you my three reasons for disagreeing, and where I think he's right.

So what do you think? Should we shut down business schools, or fix them? Tell me in the comments.
