Is AI Killing the Entry-Level Job?

About this episode

Recorded November 12, 2026.

Microsoft just offered about 60,000 employees money to leave. Barclays is cutting about 5,000 jobs, and American Express about 5% of its staff. The headlines say AI did it. So is AI killing the entry-level job, or just taking the blame?

Watch next: Is AI Killing the PhD? youtu.be

In this episode I go through the payroll data that ADP shared with Stanford, a Harvard study of millions of work histories, and the newest numbers on this summer's college graduates. Then I ask the question I ask my students about any number: compared to what? I tell you where I land. AI isn't killing the entry-level job, at least not yet. It's killing the entry-level task. And I say what that means if you're starting out, and if you hire.

The choice I can't settle: you have one open seat and a tight budget. A junior who'll be slow for a year and might leave once trained, or an experienced person who already uses AI and delivers this quarter? Which seat do you fill?
