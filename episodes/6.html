Nvidia Just Lost Jensen Huang. Now What?

About this episode

Recorded November 12, 2026.

Jensen Huang ran Nvidia for 33 years. A few days after he told his staff "I was unfaithful to my wife," he resigned as CEO and as a director. Reports say the woman worked for one of Nvidia's biggest customers, and that US officials believe she's a Chinese spy. So Nvidia just lost the only CEO it has ever had. Now what?

Watch next: Is AI Killing the PhD? youtu.be

I walk through what happened day by day, what investors call key-person risk (I call it owner risk), and what Nvidia's and Tesla's filings say about it. Then I compare Nvidia's fall with Intel in 2018 and McDonald's in 2019. My read: Intel and McDonald's lost a manager, and Nvidia lost its founder.

The choice I can't settle: make Colette Kress the permanent CEO, with no learning curve but built Huang's way, or bring in an outsider who could build the bench Huang never did, but would have to learn Nvidia with the whole market watching. Which do you pick, and what's the one reason that tips it?
