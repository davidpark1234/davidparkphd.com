About

David Park PhD

I'm a professor of entrepreneurship at Syracuse University, and I study what makes startups and businesses succeed.

Advice on starting a business is everywhere. Evidence about what actually works is harder to find, and that is what I work on. My research looks at how founders and leaders win the money, people and support a new organization needs, and how their own values shape the way they do it, in startups, nonprofits and social enterprises. It has been published in Strategic Management Journal, Journal of Business Venturing and Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal.

At the Whitman School of Management I teach strategy and entrepreneurship, including the capstone course where student teams take a business idea all the way to a plan and a pitch. I am also a fellow of Syracuse's Institute for an Entrepreneurial Society, and I travel to teach strategy and entrepreneurship around the world.

  • Poets&Quants World's Best 40 Under 40 MBA Professors
  • Poets&Quants Top 50 Undergraduate Business Professors
  • SBA Instructor of the Year

I have been on the founder's side too. I co-founded a technology startup before I became an academic, and I have also started an asset management firm and a media company. I also advise founders and companies.

I started the Evidence-Based Entrepreneurship series to put that research and experience where founders can use it. Each episode takes one question you face with a business idea, shows what the evidence says, and comes with a free worksheet for your own idea.

Before all of this I served as a captain in the Republic of Korea Marine Corps. I studied business at the University of Seoul and Seoul National University and earned my PhD in Business Administration at the University of Washington. Outside of work, I lift heavy weights and practice jiujitsu.

Short bio

David Park is an associate professor of entrepreneurship at the Whitman School of Management, Syracuse University, and a fellow of its Institute for an Entrepreneurial Society. He studies what makes startups and businesses succeed, and his research has appeared in Strategic Management Journal, Journal of Business Venturing and Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal. Poets&Quants has named him one of the World's Best 40 Under 40 MBA Professors and one of the Top 50 Undergraduate Business Professors. Before academia he co-founded a technology startup and served as an officer in the Republic of Korea Marine Corps. He holds a PhD in Business Administration from the University of Washington.
