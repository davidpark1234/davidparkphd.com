About

David Park PhD

Advice on starting and running a business is everywhere. Evidence about what actually works is harder to find, and that is what David Park studies. His research examines what makes startups and businesses succeed and has been published in top journals in strategy and entrepreneurship.

David is a professor of entrepreneurship at the Whitman School of Management, Syracuse University. He teaches strategy and entrepreneurship around the world, and his teaching has been recognized globally.

  • Poets&Quants World's Best 40 Under 40 MBA Professors
  • Poets&Quants Top 50 Undergraduate Business Professors
  • SBA Instructor of the Year

He has also started businesses himself, including a technology startup he co-founded before his academic career, an asset management firm and a media company, and he advises founders and companies.

He creates video series that put his research and experience where people can use them. The first, Evidence-Based Entrepreneurship, takes one question a founder faces in each episode, shows what the evidence says, and comes with a free worksheet.

Before all of this, he served as a captain in the Republic of Korea Marine Corps. He earned his PhD in Business Administration at the University of Washington.

Outside of work, he likes to lift heavy weights and practice mixed martial arts.
