David Park PhD

David Park is a professor of entrepreneurship at the Whitman School of Management, Syracuse University. He studies what makes startups and businesses succeed, and travels the world to teach strategy and entrepreneurship.

His research has been published in leading journals, including Strategic Management Journal, Journal of Business Venturing and Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal.

Outside the classroom, he has started several businesses, including a technology startup, an asset management firm and a media company, and he advises founders and companies.

  • Poets&Quants World's Best 40 Under 40 MBA Professors
  • Poets&Quants Top 50 Undergraduate Business Professors
  • SBA Instructor of the Year

He served as a captain in the ROK Marine Corps and earned his PhD in Business Administration from the University of Washington. Outside of work, he lifts heavy weights and practices jiujitsu.
