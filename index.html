About

David Park PhD

David Park is a professor of entrepreneurship at the Whitman School of Management, Syracuse University. He studies what makes startups and businesses succeed, and travels the world to teach strategy and entrepreneurship. He has started several businesses, including a technology startup, an asset management firm and a media company, and he advises founders and companies. He served as a captain in the ROK Marine Corps.
