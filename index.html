About

David Park PhD

David Park studies what makes startups and businesses succeed, and he teaches strategy and entrepreneurship at the Whitman School of Management, Syracuse University. His research has been published in top journals in strategy and entrepreneurship, and his teaching has been recognized globally.

He has also started businesses himself, including a technology startup, an asset management firm and a media company.
