Privacy note

What happens to your email address when you sign up.

What is collected

Only the email address you type into the sign-up form.

What it is used for

To send you an email when a new episode and its worksheet come out. It is never sold or shared for anyone else's marketing.

Who handles it

The list is kept by Kit (kit.com), the email service that sends the emails. Kit may record whether an email was opened, to help keep the list healthy.

Leaving the list

Every email has an unsubscribe link. One click removes you, and you can also reply to any email to ask for your address to be deleted.

This site itself sets no cookies and runs no tracking.

Free email

Get each new episode and its worksheet

One short email when a new episode comes out, with the worksheet that goes with it. No spam, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

By signing up you agree to receive these emails. See the privacy note.
