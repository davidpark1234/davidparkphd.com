What's inside

The Merit Test is the framework David Park developed and uses in his teaching. The book walks through it one question at a time, with the research behind each one and real companies that got it right or wrong.

  1. M
    MarketDo enough people want it, badly enough?
  2. E
    EdgeWhy would they pick yours over what they do now?
  3. R
    ReturnsWill it make enough money to be worth it?
  4. I
    ImplementationCan you build it and deliver it?
  5. T
    TeamAre you the right people to do it?

Who it's for

Anyone with a business idea who wants to find out, before betting too much on it, whether it's worth pursuing. You don't need a business background.
